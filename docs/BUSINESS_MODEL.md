# 1. Business Overview

Shri Sanwariya Road Lines is a transportation and logistics company that provides road transportation services across India.

The company operates through two primary business categories:

- Market Transportation
- Company Transportation

Transportation work is completed using either:

- Shri Sanwariya Road Lines' own fleet
- External vehicle owners hired from the market

SSRL ERP is designed to digitize the complete operational and financial workflow of the business without changing its existing working methodology.

---

# 2. Business Objective

The primary objective of Shri Sanwariya Road Lines is to provide reliable transportation services while maintaining operational efficiency and profitability.

The ERP shall support this objective by:

- Reducing manual work.
- Eliminating Excel dependency.
- Improving billing efficiency.
- Simplifying trip management.
- Maintaining accurate historical records.
- Providing operational and financial visibility.

---

# 3. Revenue Model

Revenue is generated by charging customers (Parties) a freight amount for transporting goods.

The business operates using two transportation models:

## 3.1 External Vehicle Model

For market or company trips fulfilled using external vehicles:

Revenue Flow:

Customer Freight

↓

Vehicle Owner Payment

↓

Gross Margin

Gross Margin = Customer Freight − Vehicle Rate

Operational deductions may further affect profitability.

---

## 3.2 Own Fleet Model

For trips fulfilled using Shri Sanwariya Road Lines' own vehicles:

Revenue Flow:

Customer Freight

↓

Operational Expenses

↓

Operating Profit

Operational expenses include:

- Fuel
- Driver Batta
- FASTag
- Maintenance
- Other Expenses

Operating Profit = Customer Freight − Total Operational Expenses

---

# 4. Business Categories

## 4.1 Market Transportation

Market transportation consists of individually negotiated transportation jobs.

Characteristics:

- Every trip is independent.
- Freight is negotiated individually.
- Vehicle rate is negotiated individually.
- External vehicles or own fleet may be used.
- Billing is not performed through monthly company billing.

---

## 4.2 Company Transportation

Company transportation consists of transportation work executed for registered companies.

Characteristics:

- Companies may provide multiple trips every month.
- Fixed freight rates may exist for specific routes.
- Freight rates may be modified for individual trips whenever required.
- Billing follows the company's configured billing model.

---

# 5. Parties

A Party represents a customer that provides transportation work.

Types:

- Market Party
- Company Party

Every party has:

- One GST Number
- Business Details
- Contact Information
- Billing Configuration

---

# 6. Vehicle Sources

Transportation work may be completed using two sources.

## 6.1 Own Fleet

Vehicles owned by Shri Sanwariya Road Lines.

Own fleet profitability is calculated after deducting operational expenses.

---

## 6.2 External Vehicle Owners

Vehicles hired from the market.

The ERP stores:

- Vehicle Number
- Owner Name
- Owner Mobile Number

Trips preserve historical snapshots even if directory information changes later.

---

# 7. Billing Models

The ERP supports two billing models.

## 7.1 Individual Billing

One bill corresponds to one trip.

Suitable for companies requiring separate invoices for every trip.

---

## 7.2 Consolidated Billing

One bill contains multiple trips belonging to a billing period.

Suitable for companies accepting consolidated monthly invoices.

Billing configuration is defined at the company level.

---

# 8. Payment Models

## 8.1 Standard Payment

Payments are received according to the company's normal billing process.

---

## 8.2 Bulk Payment

Some companies do not pay against individual bills.

Instead:

- Payments are received in installments.
- Outstanding amounts are maintained month-wise.
- FIFO allocation is performed automatically.
- Manual allocation is not permitted.

# 11. Operational Principles

The following principles govern the business.

- The business workflow must remain unchanged.
- Historical records must never change.
- Automation must reduce manual work.
- Accuracy is more important than automation.
- Performance is more important than visual effects.
- Every business action must be traceable.
- Billing must follow company-specific configurations.
- Payment allocation must follow approved business rules.

# 13. Success Criteria

The business model shall be considered successfully implemented when:

- Daily operations are performed without Microsoft Excel.
- All transportation workflows operate inside SSRL ERP.
- Billing is generated entirely within SSRL ERP.
- Outstanding payments are accurately maintained.
- Own fleet profitability is automatically calculated.
- Business records remain historically accurate.
- Record retrieval is significantly faster than the current Excel-based workflow.
