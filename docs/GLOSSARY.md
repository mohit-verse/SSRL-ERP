# GLOSSARY

## Party

A customer or company that hires Shri Sanwariya Road Lines for transportation services.

A Party can be:

- Market Party
- Company

---

## Market Trip

A transportation trip negotiated individually.

Market trips are not part of monthly company billing.

---

## Company Trip

A transportation trip executed for a registered company.

Company trips may be billed individually or as consolidated monthly bills depending on the company's billing configuration.

---

## POD

Proof of Delivery.

A document received after delivery confirming successful completion of a trip.

The POD is required before billing according to the approved business workflow.

---

## Driver Batta

The daily food allowance paid to drivers of Shri Sanwariya Road Lines' own fleet.

A trip may contain multiple Driver Batta entries.

---

## Gaadi Rate

The amount paid by Shri Sanwariya Road Lines to the vehicle owner for a trip.

This is different from the Freight Rate charged to the customer.
